Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Privacy Protection

We respect your privacy. WayArs is developed and operated by Arsenii Panicev (AP STUDIO), an independent developer (“we”, “us”). WayArs is designed according to Privacy by Design principles — key screen analysis and rate calculation operations are performed locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Use

Accessibility Service

ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE

The app uses this access exclusively to read order parameters (e.g., amount, distance) displayed on screen in supported delivery and taxi apps (built-in and added by user) in order to calculate order profitability. The app does not record passwords, personal data, or keystrokes. Data read through the Accessibility Service is processed locally on your device, is not sent to external servers, and is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Notifications

NOTIFICATION_LISTENER

Notification access is used solely to detect new order offers while the app is running in background.

Vehicle and Rate Data

LOCAL_ONLY

Data entered in settings (fuel consumption, fuel price, evaluation thresholds) is stored only in local device memory.

Order History

LOCAL_ONLY

For the statistics screen, the app saves on your device only the amount, currency, distance, time, calculated rates and verdict of each evaluated order, and whether you accepted it. It contains no addresses, names or other personal details and is never sent anywhere.

Installed Apps List

LAUNCHER_APPS

When you add an app to scan, the app shows the launchable apps installed on your device. The list is not stored or sent anywhere; only the apps you choose are saved locally.

Floating Overlay

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

The “display over other apps” permission is used only to show the order verdict card above the delivery or taxi app. No data is collected through it.

App Updates

GOOGLE_PLAY

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available (Google Play in-app updates API).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Privacy Protection

We respect your privacy. WayArs is developed and operated by Arsenii Panicev (AP STUDIO), an independent developer (“we”, “us”). WayArs is designed according to Privacy by Design principles — key screen analysis and rate calculation operations are performed locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Use

Accessibility Service

ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE

The app uses this access exclusively to read order parameters (e.g., amount, distance) displayed on screen in supported delivery and taxi apps (built-in and added by user) in order to calculate order profitability. The app does not record passwords, personal data, or keystrokes. Data read through the Accessibility Service is processed locally on your device, is not sent to external servers, and is not shared with third parties or used for advertising. The service never taps, types or changes anything on the screen and is not used to record calls. Events from all other apps are ignored immediately: nothing from them is read or stored. Before the service can be enabled, the app shows a disclosure and asks for your consent, and you can turn it off at any time.

Notifications

NOTIFICATION_LISTENER

Notification access is optional and is used solely to detect new order offers from the supported apps while the app is running in background. Other notifications are not read or stored.

Settings You Enter

LOCAL_ONLY

Fuel consumption, fuel price, evaluation thresholds, tax rate, partner (fleet) commission and weekly payment, currency, language and your on/off choices are stored only in local device storage.

Order history

LOCAL_ONLY

For the statistics screen the app saves, on your device only, for each order you accepted: the amount, currency, distance, time, calculated rates, estimated fuel cost, net profit, the take-home amount (after fuel and tax), the verdict and which supported app the order came from (e.g. Bolt or Uber). It contains no addresses, names or other personal details and is never sent anywhere.

Free Trial

LOCAL_ONLY

To provide the one-time free trial of scanning, the app stores on your device a counter of how long scanning has run. It is not sent anywhere.

Installed Apps List

LAUNCHER_APPS

When you add an app to scan, the app shows the launchable apps installed on your device. The list is not stored or sent anywhere; only the apps you choose are saved locally.

Floating Overlay

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

The “display over other apps” permission is used only to show the order verdict card above the delivery or taxi app. No data is collected through it.

App Updates

GOOGLE_PLAY

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available (Google Play in-app updates API).

Working Time

LOCAL_ONLY

To calculate real earnings per hour, the app stores on your device when the “Active” switch was turned on and off (kept for up to about 400 days). It is not sent anywhere.

Wayo the Helper

LOCAL_ONLY

The helper’s level and energy are calculated from your decisions on the order card and stored only on your device. Questions you type into the helper are searched locally and are not saved or sent anywhere. If you tell Wayo your name, it is stored only on your device so that he can address you.

Notifications and Watch

NOTIFICATIONS

Day and week summaries are composed on your device from the local order history. If you turn on “Verdict on the watch”, each new order is posted as a short notification (verdict, amount, distance, app name); Android and your watch’s companion app (e.g. Wear OS, Galaxy Wearable) mirror it to your paired watch under their own settings and policies. WayArs does not send this data to us or to anyone else.

Home-Screen Widget

APPWIDGET

Shows today’s take-home, number of orders and per-hour rate from local data; anyone who can see your home screen can see it. You can remove the widget at any time.

Invite Code

SHARE

The app creates a random code on your device for invitations. When you tap “Invite”, the message with the Google Play link and the code is sent only through the app you choose (e.g. a messenger). Google Play may count installs from such links in anonymous, aggregated statistics.

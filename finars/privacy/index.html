Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

We respect your privacy. The FinArs app is designed so that the user's financial and business data is stored locally on their device. The app does not have its own server for storing users' financial data.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data processed in connection with the use of the FinArs app is Arsenii Panicev, acting as an independent developer within the AP STUDIO project.

For questions regarding privacy and data processing, you can contact us at: apstudiomobile@gmail.com

2. Data stored locally on the device

The basic data entered by the user into the FinArs app is stored locally on their device and is not sent to the controller's servers.

This applies in particular to:

  • income and expense data;
  • amounts, dates, categories and descriptions of transactions;
  • invoice data and counterparty data, including NIP numbers (Polish tax identification numbers);
  • data on products and inventory levels;
  • stocktaking data;
  • photos of receipts attached to transactions;
  • locally created reports and other data related to the user's business activity.

The controller does not maintain its own cloud database containing the above information.

If the user exports or backs up data on their own, the file is saved in a location on the device chosen by the user, or in a location the user chooses when sharing or saving the file. The app does not use the automatic Android system backup for its data.

Invoice PDF files generated in the app are saved automatically in the Documents/FinArs/Invoices folder on your device, so other apps that you allow to access your files may be able to read them. You can delete them at any time with your file manager.

3. PIN code and app security

If the user protects the app with a PIN code, the code is not stored in plain form. Only its cryptographic hash, together with a random salt, is saved in the app's storage on the device. The PIN code and its hash are not sent to the controller's servers.

Biometric data, such as a fingerprint or face recognition data, is not collected or stored by FinArs. Biometric authentication is handled by the device's operating system, and the app receives only the result of the verification.

4. External services

FinArs uses external services necessary for handling payments, subscriptions, ads and searching for product names by barcode. These services may process certain technical information, identifiers and purchase-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google Play Billing

FinArs Pro subscription purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. FinArs does not receive the user's payment card data. Payment information is processed by Google in accordance with Google Play's terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat

FinArs uses the RevenueCat service to manage the status of the FinArs Pro subscription.

RevenueCat may process, among other things, an anonymous app user identifier generated by the service, technical information about the device (e.g. IP address, device model, system version) and information related to the purchase and subscription status, such as activation, expiry or trial period.

RevenueCat does not receive the financial and business data stored locally in FinArs, such as income, expenses, invoices or counterparty data.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Google AdMob and Google UMP

FinArs may display ads through Google AdMob to users who do not have an active Pro subscription.

Depending on device settings, ad configuration and the consents given, Google AdMob may process, among other things, the device's advertising identifier, IP address, device information, diagnostic data and information related to the display of and interaction with ads.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to obtain and manage the user's consent to certain ways of processing data, in accordance with applicable law. Consent can be changed or withdrawn in the app settings, where this option is available, and in the device's ad settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Open Food Facts

In the inventory module, after scanning a product barcode, FinArs may query the open Open Food Facts database for the product name. For this purpose, the number of the scanned barcode is sent to the Open Food Facts service. Along with the request, the service technically receives the device's IP address.

No other data from the app (e.g. prices, inventory levels, invoice or counterparty data) is sent to Open Food Facts. If the request fails or the product is not found, the user can enter the name manually. The search takes place only in response to the user scanning a code.

Open Food Facts Privacy Policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Google Play (app updates)

FinArs may ask Google Play whether a newer version of the app is available (Google Play in-app updates API). No financial or business data from the app is sent for this purpose.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Financial data and data shared with external services

FinArs does not share with advertising services or the payment system the data entered by the user into the app's local database, such as:

  • income and expenses;
  • invoice data;
  • counterparty data;
  • NIP numbers;
  • product data;
  • inventory levels;
  • photos of receipts.

This data remains locally on the user's device, except where the user exports, shares or sends it outside the app on their own. The only information related to the inventory module that leaves the device is the number of the scanned barcode sent to Open Food Facts, as described in section 4.

6. Legal bases for processing

To the extent that FinArs processes personal data, it does so on the following legal bases (GDPR):

  • handling the FinArs Pro subscription — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract), carried out by Google Play and RevenueCat;
  • personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), collected through Google UMP;
  • searching for a product name by barcode — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing a function requested by the user);
  • contact with the controller (e-mail messages) — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding) or point (b), where the contact concerns a contract;
  • ensuring security and establishing or defending legal claims — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

7. Transfer of data outside the European Economic Area

The service providers listed in section 4, in particular Google and RevenueCat, may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers take place on the basis of mechanisms provided for in the GDPR and used by these providers, for example a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Details can be found in the privacy policies of the individual providers.

FinArs does not transfer the user's locally stored financial and business data outside the EEA.

8. App permissions

FinArs may use the following device permissions:

Camera — may be used to scan product barcodes and take photos of receipts.

Notifications — may be used to remind you of invoice payment deadlines, limits and other events related to the operation of the app.

Storage / file access — on devices and system versions that require it, file access may be used to save, read or export backups, reports and other files created by the user.

Internet access — the Internet connection is used only by features that require communication with external services: handling subscriptions, ads and searching for product names by barcode (Open Food Facts).

FinArs does not use the Internet connection to send the user's local financial database to the controller's server.

9. Data retention and deletion

Financial and business data stored locally in the app remains on the user's device until the user deletes it, clears the app data or uninstalls the app, subject to data that the user has previously exported or shared outside the app on their own.

The user can manage local data from within the app, including deleting data using the available FinArs features.

Messages sent to the controller's e-mail address are kept for the time necessary to handle the request and, where needed, for a period allowing the establishment or defense of legal claims.

Data processed by external providers, such as Google, RevenueCat or Open Food Facts, is subject to the retention and deletion rules set by those providers and to applicable law.

10. User rights

To the extent that the GDPR applies to the processing of the user's personal data, the user may in particular have the right to:

  • access their data;
  • rectify data;
  • erase data;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal).

For data stored exclusively locally, the user can manage it on their own using the features available in the app or the device settings.

For data processed by external providers, user rights may also be exercised in accordance with those providers' rules.

The user also has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, in particular with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland.

11. Children

FinArs is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal data of children below that age.

12. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures aimed at protecting the data processed in connection with the use of FinArs.

At the same time, please keep in mind that no method of data storage or transmission over the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example in the event of changes to the app's functionality, the external services used or applicable law.

The current version of the Privacy Policy will be published in a place where the user can read it. The date of the last update is given at the beginning of the document.
